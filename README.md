# 🐍 Snake Game

A classic Snake game built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Play it directly in your browser!

## Features

- 🎮 **Classic Gameplay**: Navigate the snake to eat food and grow longer
- 📊 **Score Tracking**: Keep track of your current score and personal high score (stored in browser)
- ⚡ **Progressive Difficulty**: Game speed increases as you progress
- ⏸️ **Pause/Resume**: Take a break anytime during gameplay
- 📱 **Responsive Design**: Works on desktop and mobile devices
- 🎯 **Smooth Controls**: Use arrow keys or WASD to control the snake

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Use **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** to control the snake's direction
3. Eat the red food to grow and score points
4. Avoid hitting the walls or yourself
5. Try to get the highest score!

## Controls

- **Arrow Keys** or **WASD**: Move the snake
- **Pause Button**: Pause/Resume the game
- **Reset Button**: Start a new game

## Game Rules

- The snake starts with a length of 1 segment
- Each food eaten adds 10 points to your score
- The game speed increases slightly with each food eaten
- Game ends when the snake hits a wall or itself
- Your high score is automatically saved in your browser
